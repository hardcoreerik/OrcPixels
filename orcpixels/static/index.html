Playlist editor (on-device memory)

Presets are stored in the sign's program slots (1..100) and the sign cycles them by itself, no PC needed. Any live send (preset/text/image, Auto status, OrcSDR Live) pauses it; press Play on sign to resume. Durations are saved but the protocol has no duration field (the device decides).
